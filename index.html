          3. Agents that come with your areas

          Each chosen area lists its agents. Microsoft has published credit rates for only a few, so for the rest enter an estimate in credits a month, for example from Microsoft’s Copilot Credit Estimator. Copilot Studio is the last group, for voice, apps and custom agents.

          4. How you buy credits

          An estimate, not a quote. Prices are USD list prices, subject to change, and licences are billed annually. Credits bundled with Premium licences are pooled across the tenant. Capacity packs are billed annually, unused credits do not roll over and usage above purchased capacity can be blocked, so keep pay-as-you-go for the overflow. Not included: Dataverse storage add-ons, Teams Phone, Customer Voice add-ons and other add-on capacity. Sales Order Agent credits use 14 per request plus 5 when an attachment has sales data, which reproduces Microsoft’s 1,650-credit example but is my reconstruction. Agents with no published rate need a figure from Microsoft’s Copilot Credit Estimator.
